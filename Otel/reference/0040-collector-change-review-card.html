Collector Change Review Card

每一層測不同 failure；通過上一層不代表下一層

  1. Resolve/validate：exact binary、final config、schema、reference graph。
  2. Fixtures：representative/missing/wrong-type/sensitive/high-cardinality inputs。
  3. End-to-end：三 signals、routing、redaction、relationships、freshness。
  4. Load：steady/peak/burst、items+bytes、CPU/memory/queue、backend limits。
  5. Failure：backend outage、throttle、restart、network partition、state rebalance。
  6. Rollout：canary cohort、old/new comparison、minimum observation window。
  7. Rollback：trigger、authority、known-good artifact、state/queue treatment。
